Savings that grow with you

Saving regularly keeps your money safe, earns you interest and builds the record you need to borrow.

Savings

Regular savings

Save weekly or monthly, any amount you can manage. Your savings are kept safely with clear records, and they grow over time.

  • Save on a schedule that suits your income
  • Builds your savings history for future loans
  • Clear statements of what you have saved
Ask about savings

Groups

Group savings & loans

Farmer groups and workers’ associations can save and borrow together, with one shared, transparent account the whole group can follow.

  • One account for the whole group
  • Members support and guarantee each other
  • Open records the group can check at any time
Ask about group accounts

Borrow

Loans that follow the season

Fair credit for farming and family life, with repayment plans agreed with you before you borrow.

Loan

Farm input loans

Borrow for seed, fertiliser, feed or tools, and repay after you harvest or sell. Sized to fit your garden or plantation.

  • Seed, fertiliser, feed and tools
  • Repay after harvest or sale
  • Most do not need a land title
Ask about input loans

Loan

Development loans

Bigger goals need bigger support: a dairy cow, a water tank, a storage store, or a small agribusiness of your own.

  • Livestock, water and storage
  • Starting or growing an agribusiness
  • Longer repayment periods

Emergency loans

Quick help for the moments you cannot plan for, like school fees due tomorrow or a sudden medical bill.

  • School fees and medical bills
  • Processed quickly
  • Short, simple repayment

How to apply

Getting a loan, step by step

Loans are for members. Here is what the process looks like once you have joined and started saving.

  1. 01

    Be an active member

    Join the SACCO, buy your shares and save regularly. Your savings history supports your loan.

  2. 02

    Talk to our office

    Tell us what you need the loan for. We will explain the options and what you need to bring.

  3. 03

    Apply with guarantors

    Fill the loan form with your plan and your guarantors, usually fellow members who know you.

  4. 04

    Review and repayment plan

    The Credit Committee reviews your application, and we agree a repayment plan that fits your season.
