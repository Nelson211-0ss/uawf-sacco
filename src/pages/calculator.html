Reading your results

What the numbers mean

Monthly repayment

The estimated amount you would pay each month. Your actual plan can follow your harvest, so talk to us if monthly payments do not suit you.

Total interest

The cost of borrowing. A shorter repayment period usually means less interest in total, but higher payments each month.

Save first, borrow later

Regular savings build the record that supports your loan application, and they earn interest while they wait.
