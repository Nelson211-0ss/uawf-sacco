Joining UAWF SACCO takes four simple steps. Once you are a member, you own a share of the SACCO, you can save and borrow, and you have a vote.

How to join

Four simple steps

You can start at our office in Mbarara City. Our staff will guide you through each step.

1

Visit or contact the office

Come to our office in Mbarara City, or call us, to learn more and ask questions.

2

Fill the application form

Complete a short membership form with your basic details.

3

Pay entrance fee & buy shares

A single entrance fee and a small number of shares make you an owner of the SACCO.

4

Start saving

Begin your regular savings, weekly or monthly, whatever suits your income.

Requirements

Who can join, and what to bring

Who can join

  • Smallholder farmers
  • Livestock keepers
  • Farm, plantation and dairy workers
  • Agro processing workers
  • Farmer groups and associations
  • Any Ugandan aged 18 years and above

What to bring

  • National ID
  • Two passport size photos
  • Next of kin details
  • LC1 letter (if requested)

Benefits

What membership gives you

A SACCO is owned by its members. That means the benefits stay with the people who save and borrow here.

A safe place to save

Your savings are kept with clear records in a society registered and supervised by the Registrar.

Loans that suit farming

Borrow for inputs, livestock or emergencies, with repayment that follows your season.

You own a share

Your shares make you a part owner. Any surplus benefits members, as agreed at the general meeting.

A vote that counts

One member, one vote. Elect the board and committees and help set SACCO policy.

Free training

Sessions on financial literacy, record keeping, good farming practice and markets.

A community behind you

Fellow members who support each other through good seasons and hard ones.

Fees & shares

Entrance fee and shares

New members pay a one-time entrance fee and buy a small number of shares. The current amounts are set by members at the general meeting, so please ask our office for the latest figures before you come.
